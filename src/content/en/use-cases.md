## Not sure if Ice Box is right for you? {#fit}

Ice Box fits this situation well: you have some apps you need now and then and don't want to uninstall, but you don't want them running in the background or sitting on your home screen the rest of the time.

If an app needs to keep working in the background, such as a chat app that must receive messages in real time, an alarm clock or navigation, it is not a good candidate for freezing.

Ready? First [choose a work mode](/guide/#modes), then [download Ice Box](/download/).

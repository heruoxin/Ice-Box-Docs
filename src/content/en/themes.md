## How to import {#import}

On a phone with Ice Box installed, tap "Import to Ice Box", or copy the theme link and tap "Import from clipboard" in Ice Box Settings.

Not happy with it? In Ice Box settings, open "Theme & Layout" and use the menu in the top right corner to restore the default.

## Create your own {#create}

You are welcome to use the theme tools in Ice Box to create good-looking color schemes. An Ice Box theme sets colors for these parts: the freeze button, accent color, toolbar, background, status bar and bottom buttons. You can also set the "Icon Size" and choose an "Icon Pack".

> [!TIP]
> Know your way around design, and find the built-in Material Design palette too limiting? Tap the theme panel 10 times in a row to open the ARGB editor, where you can enter your own ARGB values for each color, including transparency.

When you are done, share the theme from "Theme & Layout" to get a link that starts with `icebox.catchingnow.com/theme/`, and send it to your friends.
